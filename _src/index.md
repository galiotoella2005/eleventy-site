Have you ever wondered about the privacy of your data? In todays age we all have various forms of technology, iPhones, iPads, computers, smartwatches, airpods, vr headsets, etc. etc. All forms of technology gather some kind of information from users, typically listed in the 'Privacy Policy' that we so often click through. This guide is to inform you about the kind of data that your devices are collecting, specifically wearable devices. Dont be alarmed! There are tips to protect your data as well.
